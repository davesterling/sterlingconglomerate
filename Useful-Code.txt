Useful Conventions.

Units of measurement

	1	Pixels (px) - a fixed unit of measurement that represents a single dot on a screen.
	2	Percentage (%) - a unit of measurement relative to the size of the parent container.
	3	Viewport Width (vw) - a unit of measurement relative to the width of the viewport.
	4	Viewport Height (vh) - a unit of measurement relative to the height of the viewport.
	5	em - a unit of measurement relative to the font size of the element.
